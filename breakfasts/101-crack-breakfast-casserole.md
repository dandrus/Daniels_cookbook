## Crack Breakfast Casserole
*Fresh Savory*

Servings: 12 | Prep: 25 min | Cook: 1 hr 5 min | Total: ~1 hr 40 min

### Ingredients
- 30 oz frozen shredded hash browns, thawed
- 12 strips thick-cut bacon, cooked crispy and crumbled
- 1 lb breakfast sausage
- 12 large eggs
- 8 oz cream cheese, softened
- 3 cups shredded sharp cheddar cheese
- 1 packet (1 oz) ranch seasoning mix
- 4 green onions, sliced
- ½ cup whole milk
- 1 tsp salt
- ½ tsp black pepper
- 2 Tbsp butter (for greasing)

### Instructions
1. Thaw hash browns completely and squeeze handfuls to remove excess moisture.
2. Butter a 9×13 baking dish and spread the squeezed hash browns evenly across the bottom.
3. Bake hash browns at 400°F for 20 minutes, until edges turn golden.
4. Meanwhile, cook bacon until crispy; drain and crumble. Brown sausage in the same skillet, breaking it into small pieces, then drain all grease.
5. In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, ranch seasoning, salt, and pepper.
6. Microwave the softened cream cheese for about 40 seconds, then whisk into the egg mixture until mostly smooth.
7. Reduce oven to 350°F. Scatter bacon and sausage over the pre-baked hash browns.
8. Pour the egg mixture evenly over the meat and potatoes, reaching all corners.
9. Sprinkle generously with cheddar cheese and top with sliced green onions.
10. Bake at 350°F for 45–50 minutes, until the center is set and a knife inserted comes out clean.
11. Let rest 10 minutes before serving.

### Notes / Tips
- Assemble through step 9, cover, and refrigerate overnight for a make-ahead breakfast; add a few minutes to the bake time.
- Squeezing the hash browns dry is key to avoiding a soggy casserole.
- Swap in spicy sausage or add a pinch of cayenne for extra heat.
- Leftovers reheat well in the microwave or a 325°F oven.
