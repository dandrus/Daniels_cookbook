## Angie's Breadsticks
*Angie*

Servings: 15–20 breadsticks | Prep: 15 min | Rise: 20 min | Cook: 25–30 min | Total: ~1 hr

### Ingredients
- ⅓–½ cup butter
- 1½ cups warm water
- 1 Tbsp yeast
- ¾ tsp salt
- 2–3 Tbsp honey
- 3½ cups flour
- Garlic salt, to taste

### Instructions
1. Melt the butter in a 9×13 pan.
2. Dissolve yeast in the warm water, then add honey and salt; mix well.
3. Knead in enough flour to form a soft but not sticky dough.
4. Roll the breadsticks out in your hands and lay them in the pan, rolling each in the melted butter as you go. Squish them together to fit 15–20 in the pan.
5. Top with garlic salt or garlic spread.
6. For best results, let rise about 20 minutes while the oven preheats (you can bake immediately if short on time).
7. Bake at 350°F for 25–30 minutes, until golden.

### Notes / Tips
- If using whole wheat flour, add a little more water and slightly less flour.
- Letting the dough rise gives a lighter, fluffier breadstick.
- Great alongside soups, pasta, or chili.
